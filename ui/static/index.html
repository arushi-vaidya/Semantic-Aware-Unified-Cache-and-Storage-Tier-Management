How it works

1 · Features

30 clues per request from past history only: gaps between accesses, access counts, scan rate.

2 · Survival model

LightGBM predicts the chance of reuse in 12 time windows → a full survival curve S(t).

3 · Conditional score

P(reuse within H | idle for e). Blocks that don't come back on time lose value automatically.

4 · One decision

Same score decides: keep in DRAM, demote to SSD, or leave on HDD (no wasted flash writes).

Model & method

What the model looks at

Share of LightGBM gain, averaged over traces

Parameters

The scoring rule

pT(x) = P(reuse within HT | idle for e) = 1 − Sx(e + HT) / Sx(e)

Sx is the predicted survival curve; e is how long the block has been idle; HT is the tier's turnover time (capacity ÷ insertion rate × 0.5). Evict the lowest score among 64 sampled blocks; admit a new block only if it beats the victim; demote a DRAM victim to SSD only if it beats the SSD victim.
